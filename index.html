How to Play

Red goes first. Regular checkers move diagonally forward one square. Jump over an opposing checker into an empty square to capture it. Captures are required when available, and additional jumps must be completed when available.

Each attempted move requires a question. Correct answers complete the move. Incorrect answers end that player's turn without moving. Kings can move and capture diagonally in either direction.
